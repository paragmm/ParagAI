Artificial intelligence has evolved rapidly with the introduction of deep neural networks.
The Transformer architecture, introduced in 2017, replaced recurrent neural networks as the foundation of modern NLP.
At the heart of the Transformer lies the multi-head self-attention mechanism.
Self-attention allows the model to weigh the relevance of different words in a sentence simultaneously.
Decoder-only transformers, such as GPT and LLaMA, predict tokens autoregressively from left to right.
Rotary Position Embeddings (RoPE) encode relative position information directly into query and key projections.
Root Mean Square Normalization (RMSNorm) normalizes activation tensors efficiently without computing mean centering.
By training on extensive text corpora through next-token prediction, transformers learn syntax, grammar, and reasoning patterns.
